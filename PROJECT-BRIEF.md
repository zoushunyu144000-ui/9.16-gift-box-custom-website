## Design Status
Client target:
**Luxury + Clean**

Important clarification:
- Client supplied an AI-generated black/gold Moire Co. logo reference.
- **Client explicitly said she does NOT want a black website.**
- The black background is only part of the AI logo reference, not the website direction.

Current working art direction:
**LINE & LIGHT**

Core:
- Predominantly light warm ivory / cream
- Champagne accents
- Near-black mainly for typography / small controls
- Brand language derived from the logo's flowing M curve + single four-point star
- Real premium ecommerce, not a fashion portfolio or AI quiet-luxury template

## Current UI Review
Latest Stitch rounds fixed several earlier requirement problems, but the visual direction still felt too generic / AI-generated.

Main problems identified:
- Too much generic quiet-luxury editorial language
- Repetitive "text + image" section compositions
- AI-styled product photography / inconsistent packaging
- Insufficient use of actual Moire brand language
- Some invented luxury/business copy in previous iterations

Claude Code's independent art-direction review recommended:
- build from the real M mark structure instead of generic editorial styling
- keep the site light
- use one continuous line + one point of light as restrained brand devices
- prioritize product scale, clarity and consistent photography
